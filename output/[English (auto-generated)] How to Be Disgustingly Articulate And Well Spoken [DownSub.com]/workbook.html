English Summary

In this video, Vin, a communication coach, shares three frameworks to help people become more articulate and well-spoken. The first scenario is when you know your topic but can't explain it simply. He introduces a four-step loop: capture (write down your thoughts), structure (organize them), practice (speak out loud), and iterate (refine). The second scenario is when you don't know much about the topic. He suggests naming what you know, what you're still working out, and asking a question to move the conversation forward.
